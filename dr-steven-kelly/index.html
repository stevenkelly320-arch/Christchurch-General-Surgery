About Dr Kelly

Dr Steven Kelly is an Upper GI, general and bariatric surgeon at Christchurch General Surgery. He has over 20 years of experience, with subspecialty training at Addenbrooke's Hospital in Cambridge, the Edinburgh Royal Infirmary and Musgrove Park Hospital in Taunton. He has performed more than 3,000 bariatric and 10,000 keyhole procedures.

Oesophageal and gastric cancer surgery is a particular interest. Dr Kelly is a consultant surgeon at Christchurch Hospital, St George's Hospital and Southern Cross Hospital.

Where Dr Kelly operates

Dr Kelly consults at 146 Leinster Road, Merivale. Private surgery is performed at St George's Hospital and Southern Cross Hospital in Christchurch, and he also works in the public system at Christchurch Hospital.
