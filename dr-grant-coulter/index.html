About Dr Coulter

Dr Grant Coulter is now in semi-retirement after 35 years of a highly respected career as a consultant general surgeon in Christchurch. Although he no longer performs surgery, he is available to see patients for consultations, and he also performs gastroscopy and colonoscopy.

Training and career

  • 1980MBChB, University of Otago
  • 1989FRACS, Royal Australasian College of Surgeons
  • 35 yrsConsultant general surgeon in Christchurch
  • NowConsultations, gastroscopy and colonoscopy at Christchurch General Surgery

What Dr Coulter offers

  • General surgery consultations
  • Assessment of abdominal pain
  • Gastroscopy and colonoscopy
  • Referral to Dr Kelly or Dr Flint for surgery

How Dr Coulter works with the team. Dr Coulter no longer performs surgery. If your consultation or endoscopy shows that surgery is needed, he refers you directly to Dr Kelly or Dr Flint, so your care stays within the one practice.

Seeing Dr Coulter

Dr Coulter consults at 146 Leinster Road, Merivale. No GP referral is required, although one is welcome. Send a request through this page or call the rooms on 03 375 4949.
