About Dr Flint

Dr Richard Flint is an Upper GI, general and bariatric surgeon at Christchurch General Surgery. He has over 20 years of experience, with fellowship training at Brigham and Women's Hospital (Harvard) and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center in New York. He has been a consultant general and Upper GI surgeon at Christchurch Hospital since 2009.

Patients and colleagues know him for a methodical approach and clear communication. Sarcoma surgery is a particular interest.

Areas of practice

  • Keyhole gallbladder surgery for gallstones
  • Hernia repair, from groin hernias to complex abdominal wall reconstruction
  • Anti-reflux surgery and hiatal hernia repair
  • Gastroscopy and colonoscopy
  • Oesophageal and stomach (Upper GI) surgery
  • Sarcoma surgery
  • Bariatric surgery through Christchurch Weight Loss Surgery

Where Dr Flint operates

Dr Flint consults at 146 Leinster Road, Merivale. Private surgery is performed at St George's Hospital and Southern Cross Hospital in Christchurch, and he also works in the public system at Christchurch Hospital.
